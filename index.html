Day 5 · 27 May — KTX to Busan & Haeundae Coast
Day 5
Seoul → Busan
KTX to Busan · Haeundae Coastal Route
Wednesday, 27 May 2026
🏨 Stay Sai Jongno (checkout) → Best Western Haeundae Hotel ⏰ Day starts at 10.00
  • Morning: check out of Stay Sai Jongno — store or bring bags to station.
  • Head to Seoul Station — KTX No. 27 departs 11:58 · arrives Busan 14:42.
  • Afternoon Arrival: Taxi or subway (Line 2) to Haeundae. Check in to Best Western Haeundae Hotel (centrally located right near the beach).
  • Afternoon Snack: Walk over to the famous Goraesa Eomuk Fishcake Shop on the main Haeundae street. Grab some premium skewered fish cakes and hot broth—a perfect, quick first taste of Busan.
  • Coastal Walk (Westbound): Start at Haeundae Beach. Take a leisurely stroll along the 1.5km sandy stretch heading west towards the high-rises.
  • Follow the boardwalk into Dongbaekseom. This small, foliage-covered island has a highly scenic, paved coastal walkway hugging the rocky cliffs.
  • Stop by the glassy Nurimaru APEC House on the tip of the island for panoramic views of the ocean and the Gwangan Bridge.
  • Skywalk & Sunset (Eastbound): Grab a quick taxi (approx. 10 mins) to the far east end of the bay. Visit the Cheongsapo Twin Lighthouses.
  • Walk over to the Cheongsapo Daritdol Skywalk. It's a 72-meter blue-dragon-shaped pier with glass floors suspended over the ocean—ideal for late afternoon/sunset views.
  • Dinner: Head back towards the Haeundae center for Puradak (premium Korean fried chicken) near your hotel

Day 6 · 28 May — Centum City Spa Day, Gwangalli & Hotel Move
Day 6
Busan
Centum City · Spa Land · Gwangalli · OSOSO Check-in
Thursday, 28 May 2026
🏨 Best Western Haeundae (checkout) → OSOSO Busan ⏰ Day starts at 09.30
  • Morning: Check out of Best Western Haeundae Hotel. Tip: Bring luggagge to Shinsegae Department Store and use their large, free lockers to explore hands-free.
  • Explore Shinsegae Department Store, Centum City — recognized as one of the largest department stores in the world.
  • Lunch: Grab lunch at the Shinsegae food court or explore the nearby Centum City dining options.
  • Afternoon: SPA LAND Centum City — spend 3–4 hours relaxing in this massive, luxurious modern Jimjilbang located right inside Shinsegae. Features 13 themed saunas, outdoor foot baths, and natural hot spring water.
  • Evening: Walk over to Gwangalli Beach — relax on the sand, watch the sunset, and see the famous Gwangan Bridge (Diamond Bridge) light up after dark.
  • Dinner Options: Subyeon Chwego Dwaeji Gukbap (Busan's signature rich pork soup), Mipo-jib Gwangalli (famous for raw marinated seafood), or Handasot Gwangalli (premium Korean pot rice).
  • Late Evening: Retrieve your luggage (if stored at Shinsegae) and take a taxi or the subway to finally check in to OSOSO Busan in Dong-gu.
Day 7 · 29 May — Gamcheon, Nampo-dong & Seomyeon
Day 7
Busan
Gamcheon · Jagalchi · Nampo · Seomyeon
Friday, 29 May 2026
🏨 OSOSO Busan · Breakfast included ⏰ Day starts at 08.00
  • Breakfast at OSOSO Busan (included)
  • Morning: Gamcheon Culture Village — colourful hillside village, murals, art; take 마을버스 (village bus) 1-1 from Toseong-dong station.
  • Late Morning: Head down to Jagalchi Fish Market — Korea's largest seafood market. Very lively and photogenic, located right on the water.
  • Lunch: Fresh seafood at Jagalchi, or walk across the street to Nampo-dong for milmyeon (Busan wheat noodles) or dwaeji gukbap (pork soup).
  • Afternoon: Walk through BIFF Square (Busan International Film Festival walk of fame) and explore Gukje Market (국제시장) — sprawling indoor market for clothes, street food, and local goods.
  • Evening: Take Subway Line 2 from nearby Motgol Station north to the Seomyeon district — explore the underground shopping mall, trendy cafes, and beauty stores.
  • Late Evening: Seomyeon Pojangmacha Street — lively outdoor snack stalls.
  • Dinner in Seomyeon — tteokbokki, grilled skewers, or classic Korean BBQ.
